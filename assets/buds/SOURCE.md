# Bud art for the banner

`pdp-ash-plush-900.png`, `pdp-pearl-plush-730.png` and `pdp-sprout-plush-900.png`: Ash, Pearl and Sprout, the SkyBuds
plush, the transparent product renders behind the public product images, downscaled to 480 px on the long side with
`sips -Z 480` (file names kept). `scripts/render.mjs` draws the banner (`dist/banner-*.svg`) from them.
https://getskybuds.com/cdn/pdp-ash-plush-900.webp, https://getskybuds.com/cdn/pdp-pearl-plush-730.webp and
https://getskybuds.com/cdn/pdp-sprout-plush-900.webp (public product images, 200 logged out, 2026-10-20).
